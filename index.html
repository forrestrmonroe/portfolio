Biography

Forrest Monroe specializes in the complete lifecycle of sound production. From the initial recording sessions to the final mastering touches, his focus is on delivering clear, immersive audio experiences.

His expertise spans across Mixing, Mastering, Recording, and Restoration. Whether it's balancing multi-track arrangements to achieve the perfect mix, ensuring a track is sonically optimized for various playback systems during mastering, or salvaging and restoring legacy audio recordings to their former glory, Forrest brings a meticulous ear and technical precision to every project.

Skills

Mixing

Forrest approaches mixing as an art form, much like songwriting and production. Starting with the fader, he balances each element to support the artist’s intent and bring clarity, depth, and impact to the music.

Mastering

Forrest’s mastering process polishes and strengthens a recording’s sound, balancing tone and loudness so the music translates across listening systems.

Recording

Forrest captures performances through careful microphone placement and recording techniques suited to each artist and instrument.

Restoration

Forrest restores legacy or damaged recordings with specialized tools, reducing unwanted noise while preserving the character of the original audio.

Transcription

Forrest transcribes audio content into clear, accurate written text.

History

Forrest Raymond Monroe is a freelance mixing and mastering engineer born in 2003, Saranac Lake, New York. Growing up alongside a musically vibrant town and family, he was exposed to various types of music at a young age. His two older brothers had drastically different musical appetites, which gave Forrest insight into the universal impact of music on people's emotions.

From even his youngest years Forrest participated alongside his family in musical theater. He is a gifted vocalist and spent many of his teenage years performing in vocal-based competitions within the U.S.

Fostering an interest in computers from a young age, Forrest’s primary interests culminated in the intersection of emotion and technology.
